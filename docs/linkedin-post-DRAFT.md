I am building something new.

KidneyScan AI.

It finds kidney tumors and cysts in 3D CT scans.

Upload a CT.
The AI finds the kidney, tumor, and cyst.
It shows the result in 3D.
It also gives tumor volume in cm³.

The data is real.
KiTS23 — 599 cases.
489 training cases, public.
Labels: kidney, tumor, and cyst.

The model is a light 3D U-Net, built in PyTorch.
Same style as my NeuroScan AI project, but for the kidney.

My targets:
Kidney Dice above 0.95.
Tumor Dice above 0.80.
Cyst Dice above 0.65.

This is still in progress.
The demo is not live yet.
I will share it when it is ready.

Why I am building it:
Small hospitals in Pakistan often have no kidney specialist.
Students need real CT projects to learn from.
Doctors need a fast second check before surgery planning.

One honest note:
This is an assistive tool only.
A doctor must confirm every result.
It is not a final diagnosis.

Code and demo will be free and open.
More updates soon.
