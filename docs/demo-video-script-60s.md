# KidneyScan AI — 60-Second Demo Video Script

Total: 60 seconds.
Style: like NeuroScan v2.
Short beats. Clear on-screen text.
No voiceover needed, or a short AI voiceover.

## Beat 1 — Title (0–7s)

Visual: black screen, then the KidneyScan AI logo.
A 3D kidney fades in slowly.

On-screen text:
"KidneyScan AI"
"Find kidney tumors in 3D CT"

## Beat 2 — Upload a CT (7–15s)

Visual: mouse drags a CT file (.nii.gz) into the demo.
Upload bar fills fast.

On-screen text:
"Upload a CT scan"

## Beat 3 — Run the model (15–25s)

Visual: "Run Model" button is clicked.
A short loading animation.
The 3D scan builds up, slice by slice.

On-screen text:
"Real AI runs on your scan"
"No hard-coded highlight"

## Beat 4 — 3D view, rotate (25–38s)

Visual: 3D kidney rotates 360 degrees.
Left panel: original scan, no overlay.
Right panel: kidney in grey, tumor in yellow/red, cyst in blue.
User drags to rotate and zooms in.

On-screen text:
"Kidney, tumor, and cyst in 3D"
"Rotate. Zoom. Check every angle."

## Beat 5 — Results and volumes (38–48s)

Visual: results panel slides in.
Tumor volume in cm³.
Cyst count and size.
Location: left kidney or right kidney.

On-screen text:
"Tumor volume: in cm³"
"Size and location, ready for the doctor"

## Beat 6 — Normal case, no highlight (48–53s)

Visual: a second scan, a normal kidney.
The model runs. Nothing is highlighted.

On-screen text:
"Normal scan? No highlight."
"The model decides, not a dropdown."

## Beat 7 — Free and open (53–60s)

Visual: GitHub repo page and the demo site.
Logo fades back in.

On-screen text:
"Free. Open. For everyone."
"Built by Wajid Ali — NUML"
"GitHub: wajidcodes"
