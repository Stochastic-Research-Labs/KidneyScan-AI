# How KidneyScan AI Helps Others

KidneyScan AI is free and open.
It is built to help people, not just to learn.

## Small hospitals in Pakistan

Many small hospitals have no kidney specialist.
A CT scan can wait days for a report.
KidneyScan AI gives a fast second look.
It finds the kidney, tumor, and cyst in 3D.
A doctor can check the result in minutes.
It does not replace the doctor.
It helps the doctor work faster.

## Doctors who want a second check

A doctor can upload a CT and compare.
The model shows the tumor and cyst clearly.
It gives tumor volume in cm³.
This helps with surgery planning.
It shows the tumor size and location.
Left kidney or right kidney.
It is a second opinion from AI.
The doctor makes the final call.

## Students learning CT segmentation

Students can see real 3D medical AI.
The code is public on GitHub.
The Kaggle notebook is public too.
They can train the model step by step.
They can test it on new CT scans.
They learn kidney, tumor, and cyst labels.
They learn 3D U-Net, PyTorch, and CT data.
No paid tools are needed.

## Researchers

The full pipeline is open.
Data source, model, and results are shared.
Others can improve the model.
Others can test it on their own data.
The target Dice scores are public.
So the work can be checked honestly.

## What it does NOT do

- It does not give a final diagnosis.
- It does not replace a doctor.
- It does not treat patients.
- It is not for commercial use.
- The data license is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
- Free for research only.

## The honest rule

Assistive only.
A doctor must confirm every result.
If the model says normal, a doctor still checks.
If the model finds a tumor, a doctor still confirms.
Health decisions always need a human doctor.
